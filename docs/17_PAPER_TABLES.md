*No method attains ≥ 0.945 **uniformly in `ρ`**, which is H2's condition.

to 0.9128 at `ρ = 0.8`. That non-monotonicity is precisely why a selection rule is not
a substitute for a correct variance. H2's guard — three cross-fitting schemes and three
thresholds — is met: every one falls below 0.945 somewhere.*
